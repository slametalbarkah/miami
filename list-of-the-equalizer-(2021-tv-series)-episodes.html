List of The Equalizer (2021 TV series) episodes

The Equalizer is an American crime drama television series that premiered on CBS on February 7, 2021. The series centers around Robyn McCall, an enigmatic woman in New York City and single mother to teenage daughter Delilah with a mysterious background who uses her extensive skills to help those with nowhere else to turn, acting as a guardian angel and a defender for those who cannot defend themselves while pursuing her own redemption. In May 2022, the series was renewed for its third and fourth seasons. The fourth season premiered on February 18, 2024. The fifth season premiered on October 20, 2024.

As of April 27, 2025, 73 episodes of The Equalizer have aired, currently in its fifth season.
